# AXF-135 — Apply the whole available source and route the economic role of the link

Second slice of AXF-28, on top of the AXF-134 link flow and the AXF-140 candidate facts, under `AXF-SOURCE-LINK@1.0.0`. Realization is still delegated to the AXF-25 contract (`ALT_CLS_RealizationService.apply`).

## Components

| Component                                                                           | Role                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `ALT_CLS_SourceLinkService.CandidateView.sourceResidualAfter`                       | Available amount left **on the source** once the candidate's obligation is settled — the counterpart of AXF-140's `residualAfter` (what the obligation still needs). Non-zero only when the source is larger than the obligation.                                                    |
| `ALT_CLS_SourceLinkService.ConfirmInput.economicRole`                               | Required declaration of the role: `APPLICATION`, `INVOICE_PAYMENT`, `TRANSFER`, `REFUND`. Missing or unlisted → `INVALID_ECONOMIC_ROLE`; listed but not routable here → `UNSUPPORTED_ECONOMIC_ROLE`. Both are thrown before any read of a candidate and before any write.            |
| `ALT_CLS_RealizationService.Input.economicRole`                                     | The declared role joins the canonical payload of the application, so the same operation key with another role answers `CONFLICT` instead of replaying. A caller that declares no role keeps the pre-AXF-135 fingerprint byte for byte (same pattern as the AXF-141 reversal reason). |
| `aXF_LWC_sourceLink` (review step) + `AXF_SourceLink_economicRole/_roleHelp/_role*` | The review declares the role explicitly. Nothing is preselected and nothing is inferred, so an invoice payment can never be created by omission. The candidate cell states the source amount the obligation cannot absorb.                                                           |

## Rules

- **Whole application**: the suggestion stays "source remaining, capped by the obligation remaining" (AXF-134). The obligation settles in full when the source covers it (`residualAfter = 0`) and stays PARTIAL when it does not — `PARTIAL`/`FULL` describe the settlement of the obligation, never an arbitrary split of the source.
- **Multi-target need (AXF-30)**: what the selected obligation cannot absorb is reported as `sourceResidualAfter` and is never split over several targets here, and never met by increasing the obligation (`UNAVAILABLE_AMOUNT`, plan magnitude untouched). The remainder simply stays available on the fact for a further link.
- **Existing allocations preserved, zero availability blocks**: the residual is always `amount − net`, so a partial link leaves the earlier allocation and its remainder intact; at zero the fact is `SOURCE_EXHAUSTED`, leaves the eligible list and no further allocation is accepted.
- **The role is declared, never classified**: no description rule, provider category or `AXF_CCT_TXT_BillReference__c` value produces a role. `BillReference` is the statement (Pluggy `billId`) a card line belongs to and never the payment of that statement; a fact that looks like an invoice payment stays an ordinary linkable source and still needs the caller's declaration.
- **Routing**: `APPLICATION` is the only role this slice can route — the fact settles the target's obligation, or becomes the realized entry itself. There is no supported invoice/transfer/refund link capability in this branch, so those declarations are refused without writing (`UNSUPPORTED_ECONOMIC_ROLE`): an invoice payment never creates a second consumption expense and never writes a partial effect.
- **Direction preserved**: eligibility, matching and the confirmation are equally open to an inflow (revenue) and an outflow (expense); the entry born from the fact keeps the direction the source carries.
- **Naming**: `REFUND` is the link's own role name, distinct from `REVERSAL` on `AXF_OBJ_ReconciliationAllocation__c.AXF_RA_PKL_Kind__c` (the AXF-141 compensation effect) and from the `ALT_CLS_TransactionObservationService` exclusion reasons, which are a different contract (observation eligibility, AXF-109).
- **Security** is unchanged: capability + holder checks, `WITH USER_MODE` / `AccessLevel.USER_MODE`, sanitized `{code}` errors.

## Validation

- AXON_DEV: `ALT_CLS_SourceLinkServiceTest` 16/16 (service 95 %, controller 88 %): whole application with the multi-target remainder and no obligation increase, existing allocation preserved with the remainder re-offered, zero availability (`SOURCE_EXHAUSTED`), missing/unlisted/unroutable roles refused with nothing persisted (no allocation, no `ACTUAL_ONLY` entry, no version bump), revenue direction preserved end to end, a card fact with `BillReference` and an invoice-payment description still an ordinary linkable source, and the declared role pinned inside the canonical payload. `ALT_CLS_RealizationServiceTest` 16/16 still green, keeping the legacy fingerprint replayable.
- Jest 8/8 for the component (`sourceLink` suite); full `test:unit` 236/236; ESLint clean.
- Install: the eight new labels exist in the org (`ExternalString`) with their pt-BR localizations; the `CustomLabels.labels-meta.xml` / `translations/` containers are **not** deployed because they would drop org-only labels.

## Out of scope (siblings)

Multi-target (N:N) allocation belongs to AXF-30; materialization, the single command and the holder-first SDC belong to AXF-136. The invoice, transfer and refund link capabilities themselves are not part of this slice, which is why the roles are refused rather than routed.
