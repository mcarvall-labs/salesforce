# AXF-135 — Apply the whole available source and route the economic role of the link

Second slice of AXF-28, on top of the AXF-134 link flow and the AXF-140 candidate facts, under `AXF-SOURCE-LINK@1.0.0`. Realization is still delegated to the AXF-25 contract (`ALT_CLS_RealizationService.apply`).

## Components

| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `ALT_CLS_SourceLinkService.CandidateView.sourceResidualAfter`                       | Available amount left **on the source** once the candidate's obligation is settled — the counterpart of AXF-140's `residualAfter` (what the obligation still needs). ## Rules

- **Whole application**: the suggestion stays "source remaining, capped by the obligation remaining" (AXF-134). The obligation settles in full when the source covers it (`residualAfter = 0`) and stays PARTIAL when it does not — `PARTIAL`/`FULL` describe the settlement of the obligation, never an arbitrary split of the source.
- **Multi-target need (AXF-30)**: what the selected obligation cannot absorb is reported as `sourceResidualAfter` and is never split over several targets here, and never met by increasing the obligation (`UNAVAILABLE_AMOUNT`, plan magnitude untouched). The remainder simply stays available on the fact for a further link.
- **Existing allocations preserved, zero availability blocks**: the residual is always `amount − net`, so a partial link leaves the earlier allocation and its remainder intact; at zero the fact is `SOURCE_EXHAUSTED`, leaves the eligible list and no further allocation is accepted.
- **The role is declared, never classified**: no description rule, provider category or `AXF_CCT_TXT_BillReference__c` value produces a role. `BillReference` is the statement (Pluggy `billId`) a card line belongs to and never the payment of that statement; a fact that looks like an invoice payment stays an ordinary linkable source and still needs the caller's declaration.
- **Routing**: `APPLICATION` is the only role this slice can route — the fact settles the target's obligation, or becomes the realized entry itself. There is no supported invoice/transfer/refund link capability in this branch, so those declarations are refused without writing (`UNSUPPORTED_ECONOMIC_ROLE`): an invoice payment never creates a second consumption expense and never writes a partial effect. The review offers only `APPLICATION`; the refusal text says the capability is not available in this version and asks for the application role, instead of pointing at a capability that does not exist here.
- **Replay before the role**: an operation key that was already applied is resolved before the role is judged, so a retry that declares no role reproduces the recorded result. Because the recorded role cannot be recovered from the recorded payload hash, such a retry is verified against the allocation that was recorded — source, target and amount — instead of against the hash; a retry that does declare a role is still compared by AXF-25, so a different declared role answers `CONFLICT`.
- **Direction preserved**: eligibility, matching and the confirmation are equally open to an inflow (revenue) and an outflow (expense); the entry born from the fact keeps the direction the source carries.
- **Naming**: `REFUND` is the link's own role name, distinct from `REVERSAL` on `AXF_OBJ_ReconciliationAllocation__c.AXF_RA_PKL_Kind__c` (the AXF-141 compensation effect) and from the `ALT_CLS_TransactionObservationService` exclusion reasons, which are a different contract (observation eligibility, AXF-109).
- **Security** is unchanged: capability + holder checks, `WITH USER_MODE` / `AccessLevel.USER_MODE`, sanitized `{code}` errors.

## Out of scope (siblings)

Multi-target (N:N) allocation belongs to AXF-30; materialization, the single command and the holder-first SDC belong to AXF-136. The invoice, transfer and refund link capabilities themselves are not part of this slice, which is why the roles are refused rather than routed.

## Pendencies (declared, not implemented in this pass)

### 1. The role gate exists only in this facade — an open AC-2 exposure

`ALT_CLS_SourceLinkService.confirm` is the only place that requires the role. The canonical write service `ALT_CLS_RealizationService.apply` still accepts a `BANK`/`CARD` allocation whose `economicRole` is null — a null or blank role is simply not part of the payload, by design — and the other entry point over it, `AXF_CLS_CTRL_Realization.apply` (also reached by the `aXF_LWC_realization` component), still creates a link with a null role. An invoice payment entering through that surface can therefore still create a consumption expense. AC-2 is guaranteed for this wizard's facade only. Closing it belongs to the slice that owns `AXF_CLS_CTRL_Realization` / `aXF_LWC_realization`, or to making the role a required part of the AXF-25 contract itself; neither is changed here.

### 2. AC-1 read literally when the source exceeds the obligation

"Apply the full revised amount of the available source" holds literally only while source ≤ obligation. `confirm` accepts an amount capped at the obligation's residual — `suggested = min(source.residual, target.residual)` and `input.amount <= suggested` (`UNAVAILABLE_AMOUNT` above it) — so when the source is larger, the excess never reaches the target and exists only as `sourceResidualAfter`, the multi-target need (AXF-30). The arithmetic is deliberately unchanged by this pass. **Question for the story author**: is the literal reading intended — the whole revised source amount must be applied, over several targets at once — or is capping at one obligation and reporting the remainder the accepted behaviour of this slice? If the literal reading is the requirement, it is AXF-30's multi-target allocation and not a change to the capping rule.

### 3. Doc/org divergence on labels and translations

This branch carries the label and translation **containers** (`CustomLabels.labels-meta.xml`, `pt_BR.translation-meta.xml`) while `AXON_DEV` is updated out of band through the Tooling API (`ExternalString` / `ExternalStringLocalization`, additively). The org holds 25 `AXF_AccessLevel_*` labels that are absent from this branch, so deploying either container would delete them; no container is deployed. Until a slice reconciles the two additively, the containers are authoritative for review and the org for runtime.
