# AXF-123 — Conversão cambial e neutralização de contrapartidas no consolidado

- **Jira:** https://axon-personal-finances.atlassian.net/browse/AXF-123 (parent AXF-1; split from AXF-4)
- **Branch:** `feature/AXF-123-fx-neutralization-consolidated` (base `origin/sprint/sprint-4` @ `b9ae3f7`).
- **Siblings already delivered and extended, not rebuilt:** AXF-122 (`ALT_CLS_ConsolidationService.consolidate`)
  and AXF-128 (neutralization of the internal counter-entries in that same calculation).

> Two rules only: (1) the calculation of a consolidated view changes no property and no economic
> attribution, and only **confirmed and authorized** internal counter-entries are neutralized;
> (2) two currencies are compared through the **canonical FX policy** — never a local rate, never
> parity — and a currency without a usable quote yields no number and withholds the comparable
> indicator. No new object, field, permission set, Custom Label or metadata container.

## AC-2 (b) → implementation

**The comparison is requested, never assumed.** `ScopeInput.reportingIso` is optional: blank (the
shape every existing caller uses) leaves the result byte-for-byte the AXF-122 behaviour — no
`reportingIso`, no `conversionState`, no `comparableTotal`. This is why no delivered AXF-122
assertion had to change.

**Conversion is a read of reference data, and only that.** One query returns the newest quote per
base currency **and side** for the reporting currency — the ratified provider, the layer's validity
window, `ORDER BY EffectiveDateTime DESC, Id ASC`. Nothing is written: no snapshot, no ISO code, no
magnitude. Each side is converted only if it contributes: a zero inflow or outflow needs no quote at
all, so a currency with no expense is not blocked for want of a SELL quote.

**Originals and their currency are never the thing that moves.** `Total.inflow/outflow/net` keep the
stored magnitudes and the stored ISO; the converted values live in separate
`reportingInflow/reportingOutflow/reportingNet` fields next to the applied rate, its provider and its
effective instant, so an estimate is always attributable to the quote that produced it.

**The indicator is blocked instead of guessed.** A currency whose contributing side has no usable
fresh quote gets `STALE` (present but outside the window) or `UNAVAILABLE` (absent, or the newest row
carries a non-positive rate), **no reporting numbers at all**, and the whole comparable indicator is
withheld with `MISSING_MATERIAL_FX` — a reason code already ratified by AXF-140 for exactly this
meaning, so no new vocabulary was invented. The result then stays `DEGRADED` with every original
total in place: the indicator is blocked _and_ the confidence is reduced, which is the pair the AC
asks for, and neither an assumed parity nor a partial sum can be read as the consolidated position.

**Comparability is reported as a value, not as a warning.** `Result.comparableTotal` is the only
figure in the response that mixes currencies, it is always expressed in the requested currency, and
its holder count is the **union** of the holders behind the currencies that were actually converted
(a holder present in two currencies counts once).

## AC-1 (a) → implementation

**The rules were already in place (AXF-122/128) and are now pinned where they were untested.**

- Nothing is written. The test measures `Limits.getDmlStatements()` immediately around the call and
  requires it to be unchanged, then compares the source fact, the confirmed set and its parcels
  before and after by full row serialisation (including `LastModifiedDate` and `SystemModstamp`).
  An implementation that wrote a projection, a marker or a snapshot fails that assertion.
- Only a `CONFIRMED` counter-entry is neutralized. The fixture writes a real `50/50` set through the
  AXF-126 write window as `DRAFT` and asserts that nothing is netted (the holder keeps its material
  fact, no parcel of the draft appears), then the same set is confirmed and the fact is netted once
  (two `ALLOCATION` parcels of 150, total 300).
- Only an **authorized** counter-entry is neutralized. The same confirmed set with the second
  participant left out of the requested scope keeps the fact with its holder (300), names and sums
  nothing for the outside participant, reports `ALLOCATED_OUTSIDE_SCOPE` and degrades the result.
- Attribution survives the neutralization: the holder's contribution is `ALLOCATION` with its own
  currency, its own source label and the fact's effective date.

## The judgement call (stated, with its evidence)

**Which canonical layer may convert a consolidated comparison.** The AC says the conversion "uses the
canonical snapshot/policy". Two canonical layers exist and only one of them can carry a consolidated
aggregate:

1. **The frozen snapshot (AXF-24) cannot.** `FxApplicationSnapshot` freezes a conversion **per target
   event**, its ratified target types are `FINANCIAL_TRANSACTION`, `FX_SETTLEMENT_CHARGE`,
   `CALCULATION_SNAPSHOT` (plus `RECONCILIATION_ALLOCATION`, added when that domain closed), and its
   typed lookups are exactly one per target. A bank/card movement — what the consolidated calculation
   sums — is **not** an FXS target, and the ratified carrier for a calculation snapshot
   (`AXF_OBJ_CalculationSnapshot__c` + `AXF_FXS_LKP_CalculationSnapshot__c`) is **not built in this
   branch**. Freezing the aggregate would therefore mean creating that schema, which the US does not
   scope and `AXF-4` forbids as a local invention.
2. **The canonical policy can.** The architecture assigns the reporting-currency change to _estimated_
   totals ("mudança recalculate só totais estimados") and defines the indicative layer as a
   **recomputable** equivalence that creates no snapshot and never assumes parity. That is exactly the
   shape of a comparison between two consolidated totals.

So the comparison converts through the canonical **policy** (AXF-92/G8), reusing that layer's
provider, window, display scale and state names rather than restating any of them, and it consumes no
`ExchangeRate` row for anything except this estimate — no frozen evidence is read, re-derived or
overwritten. **The alternative reading is available to the owner:** build `CSN` +
`AXF_FXS_LKP_CalculationSnapshot__c` and freeze each consolidated comparison as a ratified
calculation snapshot instead. That is a schema story, and this pass deliberately does not open it.

**No materiality threshold was invented.** The AC separates "reduces confidence" from "blocks the
indicator", and AXF-24 splits non-material assets from material liabilities — but no threshold is
ratified anywhere (`AXF_CMT_ConfidencePolicy__mdt` carries only freshness). Rather than invent a
number, a currency without a usable quote blocks the **whole** comparable indicator (so no material
obligation can vanish from a total, as AXF-24 AC-4 requires) and the confidence drops to `DEGRADED`.
If the product wants a threshold, it belongs in the confidence policy, not in this class.

## Limitations and residuals

- **The comparable indicator is fail-closed.** One currency without a usable fresh quote withholds it
  entirely, even when every other currency could be converted. That is deliberate (see above) and it
  is the honest reading of "reduz confiança ou bloqueia o indicador sem assumir paridade"; it does
  mean a scope holding an unconvertible currency gets no comparable number at all.
- **A frozen snapshot is not consumed here.** The per-event FXS remains the evidence of each material
  event; this comparison neither reads nor writes it, because the built schema has no FXS target for a
  consolidated aggregate. Documented above as the judgement call, not hidden.
- **The quote read mirrors the indicative layer, not `USER_MODE`.** The `ExchangeRate` pair is shared
  reference data and the ratified layer reads it under sharing without field-level security. This
  pass inherits exactly that posture and therefore inherits **pendency P6.2 of AXF-140** (that FLS
  posture is a recorded open decision); it does not create a new one. Every other read of this class
  stays `WITH USER_MODE`.
- **No UI surface.** The AC of this story is about the calculation; presentation is AXF-125, which
  consumes these fields. No LWC, controller or label was added, so no Jest test was added either.
- **No materiality, no tolerance, no fallback quote.** None exists in the ratified contracts and none
  was invented; an older quote is never substituted for a newer unusable one.
- **Ties on the same effective instant** are broken by record identity (`Id ASC`), following the
  AXF-140 read this pass mirrors.
- **`POLICY_VERSION` was not bumped.** The field's contract for a caller that sends no `reportingIso`
  is unchanged, and no ratified rule governs the version string of this class; bumping it would
  announce a policy amendment that did not happen.

## Review round (`/bmad-code-review`, four independent layers)

Four layers reviewed the committed delta (`blind-hunter`, `edge-case-hunter`, `verification-gap`,
`acceptance-auditor`), each reading the unified diff only. Findings **fixed** in `51d1fb6`:

1. **The reporting-currency total silently claimed "no comparison requested".** Its `conversionState`
   stayed null while a comparison had run, and the canonical layer already has `SAME_CURRENCY` for
   exactly that fact. Now set, documented and asserted.
2. **One quote instant for two applied quotes.** Only the inflow's quote instant was exposed when both
   sides converted, so the outflow's rate was unauditable. Split into
   `rateEffectiveAtInflow`/`rateEffectiveAtOutflow`, with a test asserting two different instants.
3. **A stored ISO in another case was read as "unquoted".** The totals map is keyed by the stored value
   while the reference row was matched on the normalized one. Both sides are now normalized (as the
   indicative layer does), and the fixture writes a lower-case code so the fix is load-bearing.
4. **The successful comparison never asserted confidence or reasons** (verification-gap showed that a
   reason added on the success path left every test green). Now `FULL` + empty reasons.
5. **The zero-contribution rule was pinned in only one direction** (verification-gap demonstrated that
   making both sides mandatory left all tests passing). New test for the inflow-only currency.
6. **The read's provider and window predicates could be deleted without failing anything**
   (verification-gap). New test with a competing-provider row and an out-of-window row.
7. **The class header stated `USER_MODE` for every read** while the new quote read deliberately follows
   the indicative layer's sharing-without-FLS posture, and it did not name the estimate's temporal
   anchor. Both are now stated.

Findings **discarded, with the reason recorded**:

- **"A frozen FXS should be consumed, or the estimate should be a snapshot"** (acceptance auditor).
  Kept as the documented judgement: `FxApplicationSnapshot` freezes a conversion per target event and
  the ratified calculation carrier (`CSN` + `AXF_FXS_LKP_CalculationSnapshot__c`) does not exist in
  this branch, so freezing the aggregate would mean building schema this US does not scope. The
  argument and the alternative are above; the frozen per-event snapshot is never read, re-derived or
  overwritten here.
- **"The estimate should be anchored on the fact's date with the 5-day lookback"** (acceptance
  auditor, blind-hunter). The 5-day retroactive search belongs to the material conversion of a dated
  event (AXF-24); the 7-day window is the validity of the layer whose numbers these are, and G8 itself
  assigns a reporting-currency change to _estimated_ totals. The anchor is now explicit in the header
  and every applied quote exposes its own instant.
- **"A local copy of the FX policy / a third reader of the reference table"** (both auditors,
  blind-hunter). Acknowledged as a residual, not fixed: the two existing readers answer different
  questions (a material 5-day lookback under `USER_MODE`, and a single-record display equivalence),
  and reusing one would either change AXF-140/AXF-92 behaviour or require making a private resolver
  public inside another story's merged class. Declared here so the divergence is visible.
- **"A non-positive rate should be `ESTIMATED`"** (acceptance auditor, comparing with AXF-92):
  rejected with evidence — AXF-140 (`ALT_CLS_SourceLinkService`, merged in this branch) explicitly
  refuses a non-positive quote with no substitution, and a zero rate would present `0` as a converted
  value.
- **"No materiality branch / no exclusion entry per currency"** (both auditors). The per-currency
  `conversionState` already names the currency that blocks the indicator, and no materiality threshold
  is ratified; blocking the whole indicator is the AC's allowed alternative and keeps a material
  obligation from vanishing from a total (AXF-24 AC-4).
- **"`reportingIso` is not validated, not echoed on blocked results, or not defaulted from the
  reporting-currency configuration"** (blind-hunter, acceptance auditor). Defaulting it would change
  the delivered AXF-122 contract and break its assertions, and the reporting currency is the caller's
  context (AXF-124/125 own it); an unknown code already fails closed with a typed reason.
- **"`POLICY_VERSION` should be bumped"** (blind-hunter). Rejected with evidence: the AXF-122 test
  asserts the literal `consolidation-scope@1.0.0`, and the contract of a caller that sends no
  `reportingIso` is unchanged.
- **"A future-dated quote is accepted"** (blind-hunter, edge-case-hunter). Mirrors the ratified
  indicative read exactly (no upper bound there either); adding one would be an unratified rule, and
  the alternative — substituting an older quote — is what AXF-140 forbids. The applied instant is
  exposed so a future-dated row is visible.
- **"Inverse and triangulated pairs should be read"** (blind-hunter). The mirrored indicative layer
  reads the stored direction only, and triangulation is the _material_ conversion's rule (AXF-24).
  Declared as a limitation instead of implemented.
- **"A blank ISO could suppress the indicator"** (blind-hunter). `AXF_BAT_TXT_CurrencyIsoCode__c` and
  `AXF_CCT_TXT_CurrencyIsoCode__c` are `required`, so a currency-less fact cannot be stored, and the
  outcome for one would be a withheld indicator rather than a wrong number.
- **"The comparable indicator is zero for a scope without facts"** (acceptance auditor, low
  confidence). A scope with no posted facts contributes nothing; `factCount` 0 and no contributions
  make it unambiguous, and nothing was omitted.
- **"The comparable mixes stored precision with rounded estimates"** (edge-case-hunter). Deliberate:
  rounding the reporting-currency part would lose precision the original total has, and the invariant
